HOW TO PLAY

THE LOOP

  • Clear each room by hitting its kill quota. Beat the timer for the full clear bonus — miss it and the room goes into overtime, worth half. The timer never kills you; only losing all your HP does.
  • Dashing makes you briefly invulnerable and leaves a lethal trail that keeps killing for 1.6s after the dash ends.
  • Every 3rd room opens a shop. Every 5th room is a boss — and only dash damage hurts a boss.
  • Grab the rotating diamonds: heal, haste, shield, longer dash, bonus max HP.

THE ENGINE

Five features of your play — speed, action rate, direction variance, threat proximity and idle time — are fed every tick to a non-causal attention model over a 32-frame window (16 real, 16 physics-projected).

It outputs π, a probability distribution over five psychological archetypes. π drives enemy speed and spawn mix, your friction, the camera zoom, every post-process effect, the boss's tactics — and the music.

The weights here are the exact same tensors the desktop build ships with, so the browser infers identical state.
